    Personal answers are generated by Claude and restricted to the Bhagavad Gita. The offline guide covers common situations with verses chosen in advance. Each cited verse is checked against the Sanskrit text of all 701 verses in this edition (chapter 13 counts Arjuna's opening question as 13.1). The English renderings are Claude's own, not a published translation.

    On the public website, personal answers come from Claude through Puter: your question is sent to Puter, and each visitor uses their own free Puter account and its AI allowance. The site owner pays nothing and stores nothing.

    The Gita offers perspective, not medical, legal or financial advice. If you are in danger or thinking of harming yourself, please reach someone you trust or a local crisis line now.
